# Legal and Compliance Analysis

For each issue record:

```text
Clause or requirement | Source | Jurisdiction | Obligation | Owner
Trigger | Deadline | Risk | Ambiguity | Control | Evidence | Action
```

1. Confirm document version, parties, jurisdiction and intended transaction.
2. Quote or cite the exact clause or authoritative requirement.
3. Separate mandatory obligation, negotiated position, interpretation and
   operational recommendation.
4. Identify missing definitions, conflicts, dependencies, deadlines and
   approval authorities.
5. Translate obligations into workflow, ownership, evidence and monitoring.
6. Flag issues requiring qualified counsel.
7. Never invent clauses, law or regulatory obligations.
8. Preserve confidentiality and access controls.

Use current authoritative sources for law and regulation.
