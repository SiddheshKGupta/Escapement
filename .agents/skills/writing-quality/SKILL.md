# Writing Quality

1. Lead with the point.
2. Use plain, professional, positive language.
3. Remove generic AI phrasing, repetitive cadence, inflated claims, filler, and
   unnecessary headings.
4. Distinguish fact, interpretation, recommendation, and action.
5. Keep detail proportional to the reader's decision.
6. Use business-specific nouns and verbs.
7. Preserve the active profile's voice.
8. Proofread for ambiguity, grammar, consistency, and unsupported statements.

Do not make prose sound human by making it casual; make it precise and useful.
