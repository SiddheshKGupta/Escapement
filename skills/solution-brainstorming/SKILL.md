# Solution Brainstorming

1. Confirm the decision brief and research findings.
2. Generate two or three materially different solution approaches.
3. Compare:
   - user and management outcome;
   - business-rule fit;
   - complexity;
   - risk and controls;
   - implementation effort;
   - maintainability;
   - reversibility.
4. Recommend one approach and state why.
5. Validate the proposed direction in small sections.
6. Persist the approved design before detailed planning.

Avoid cosmetic alternatives that share the same underlying design.
