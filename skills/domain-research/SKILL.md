# Domain Research

1. Read `DOMAIN_CONTEXT.md`.
2. Define the exact decisions the research must inform.
3. Search the evidence hierarchy:
   project evidence → regulator/standards body → official documentation →
   primary disclosures → institutional research → industry research →
   practitioner/community signals.
4. Record source, date, jurisdiction, applicability and confidence.
5. Distinguish:
   - mandatory requirement;
   - recognised standard;
   - common practice;
   - emerging trend;
   - opinion.
6. Use Agent Reach or Last30Days only when broader platform or recent community
   evidence is useful and approved.
7. Update `DOMAIN_CONTEXT.md`.
8. Convert findings into decisions, assumptions, requirements and risks.
