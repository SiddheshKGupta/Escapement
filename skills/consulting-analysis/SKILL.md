# Consulting Analysis

1. Define the management decision.
2. Separate facts, assumptions, interpretation, and recommendation.
3. Analyse business model, process, people, data, technology, governance, and
   implementation implications.
4. Rank findings by materiality.
5. Convert recommendations into owner, sequence, dependency, control, measure,
   and next action.
6. Challenge requests that optimise a screen without solving the operating
   problem.
7. Update decisions or requirements when the conclusion is material.

Output:

```text
Observation | Evidence | Implication | Recommendation
Priority | Owner | Dependency | Control | Measure | Next action
```
