# Governance, Risk, and Controls

1. Identify the objective and risk event.
2. Trace authority to approved policy, contract, regulation, or decision.
3. Define preventive and detective controls inside the workflow.
4. Assign owner, frequency, evidence, threshold, and escalation.
5. Define exception ageing, remediation, closure, and reporting.
6. Test segregation of duties and permission enforcement.
7. Record residual risk and open decisions.

Required model:

```text
Objective | Risk | Cause | Impact | Control | Owner | Frequency
Evidence | Exception | Escalation | Reporting | Remediation
```
