# Origins

Escapement was created to solve a recurring problem in AI-assisted delivery:

```text
Too little structure
→ the agent forgets, guesses and drifts

Too much structure
→ the agent spends its context managing process instead of delivering
```

Its universal doctrine combines decision quality, domain evidence, consulting
discipline, product engineering, security, design judgement, deterministic
verification and durable handoff.

Escapement is domain-neutral. Project-specific knowledge belongs in:

```text
DOMAIN_CONTEXT.md
PROJECT_CONTEXT.md
profiles/
docs/decisions/
```

External projects listed in the capability registry remain the property of
their respective maintainers and are used only according to their licences.
