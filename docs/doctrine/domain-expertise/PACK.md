# Domain Expertise

Use when industry, geography, regulation, professional standards, market
practice, domain vocabulary or operating norms can change the decision.

## Evidence hierarchy

```text
Project evidence
→ regulator, law or standards body
→ official vendor documentation
→ primary company disclosures
→ institutional research
→ reputable industry research
→ practitioner and community signals
```

Record source, publication date, applicability, jurisdiction and confidence.

Use current-trend sources to reveal adoption, emerging practice and pain points.
Do not treat popularity as proof of correctness.

Update `DOMAIN_CONTEXT.md` when findings materially affect the project.
