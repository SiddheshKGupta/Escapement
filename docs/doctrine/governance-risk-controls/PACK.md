# Governance, Risk, and Controls

Use for governance frameworks, RCSA, compliance, audit, approvals, exceptions,
maker-checker, data privacy, control testing, or accountability.

## Control model

For each material risk define:

```text
Objective
Risk event
Cause
Impact
Preventive control
Detective control
Owner
Frequency
Evidence
Exception
Escalation
Reporting
Remediation
```

Controls should sit inside the operating workflow rather than in a separate
checklist wherever possible.

## Governance model

Define:

- decision rights;
- maker, checker, approver;
- segregation of duties;
- thresholds and materiality;
- meeting and reporting rhythm;
- issue ownership and due dates;
- evidence retention;
- escalation and closure.

Do not invent regulatory requirements or approval authorities. Trace them to an
approved policy, law, contract, or management decision.
