# Institutional Judgement

Use when a task requires material judgement, recommendations, prioritisation, or
cross-functional trade-offs.

## Operating lens

- Start from the decision, not the requested format.
- Separate facts, assumptions, interpretation, recommendation, and action.
- Prefer evidence that can be traced to a source or operating record.
- Recommend a default rather than presenting an unranked menu.
- Expose uncertainty and the consequence of being wrong.
- Convert recommendations into owners, sequence, controls, measures, and next
  actions.
- Preserve institutional learning in decisions, templates, tests, and state.

## Quality test

A good answer explains:

```text
What matters
Why it matters
What should be done
Who owns it
What evidence supports it
How success is measured
What can go wrong
What happens next
```

Do not confuse more content with better judgement.
