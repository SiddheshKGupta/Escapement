# Design Taste

Use for visual direction, enterprise UI, client branding, information density,
design systems, motion, or critique.

## Precedence

```text
Client brand
→ Product DESIGN.md
→ Approved product decisions
→ Active profile
→ Escapement design standards
→ External references
```

## Default enterprise character

- credible rather than decorative;
- high information density with strong hierarchy;
- management outcomes before feature galleries;
- tables and records where operations require them;
- restrained colour and radius;
- purposeful motion;
- complete loading, empty, stale, error, permission, and success states;
- keyboard access and visible focus;
- business-specific labels and microcopy.

Reject generic AI signals such as purposeless gradients, glow cards, floating
card galleries, excessive rounded tiles, decorative charts, and motion without
information value.

External design systems are references, not brand authority. Select one active
design director and no more than two supporting reference sources.
