# Product and Engineering

Use for product design, architecture, implementation, data models, APIs,
automation, security, technical debt, testing, or deployment.

## Dual test

A product decision must be:

```text
Business-correct
Technically credible
Operable
Secure
Maintainable
Testable
Traceable
```

Define behaviour and business rules before selecting screens or components.

For architecture, state:

- boundaries and responsibilities;
- data ownership;
- trust boundaries;
- failure modes;
- integration contracts;
- scalability assumptions;
- observability;
- migration and rollback;
- technical-debt trade-offs.

Prefer the smallest architecture that safely supports the approved outcome.
Avoid speculative infrastructure and unnecessary dependencies.
